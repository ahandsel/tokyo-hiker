# Hinatawada Station to Mitakesan Cable Car via Mt. Hinode (日の出山) Route

* Great trail for beginners who want something a little more challenging than Mt. Takao
* Good for summer hikes as there are many shaded areas
* If you are lucky, you may spot a [Japanese serow][japanese-serow] 🦌

## Key Locations

Getting There:

* 09:25 - Take the train from [Tachikawa Station][tachikawa-station] to Ome Station
* **09:55** - Arrive at [Ome Station `青梅駅`][ome-station]
  * Buy lunch at [7-11][7-11] or [Origin Bento][origin-bento]
* **10:12** - Ome Station to Yoshino-Bairin by Bus 🚌
  * [青梅駅前 `梅０１`〔吉野・御嶽駅方面〕{都営バス} {吉野（東京都）/玉堂美術館方面} 時刻表 - NAVITIME][01-navitime]
* 10:24 - Arrive at Yoshino-Bairin Bus Stop

Start Options:

* Train 🚉 [Hinatawada Station (`日向和田駅`)][hinatawada-station]

* Trailhead 🥾 [(`日の出山 御岳山登山口`/`吉野梅郷登山口`)][ivznp39dum6zdenn9]
