Finish station: [OkuTama Station / 奥多摩駅][okutama-station] (JR Ome Line) 🚉

Travel from Shinjuku: **about 1 hr 30 min** ⏱️ <!-- TODO: verify the travel time from Shinjuku to Kori Station -->

* The walking route starts a 5-minute walk from Kori Station.


## Trail overview 🥾

* Difficulty: Easy - great for beginners! 🟢
* Toilets: Use the toilet at Kori Station before you start. 🚻
* Drink: Bring plenty of water. 🥤 <!-- TODO: verify vending machine locations along the route -->
* Lunch: Few [food options](#food-) along the trail, so pack your own. 🍱
* Hiking poles 🔧: 1 / 5
  * Not needed; the route is a riverside walkway with steps, not a mountain trail.
* Trail running 👟: 2 / 5
  * The walkway sections are runnable, but the rocky parts of Hatonosu Valley are narrow and slippery.
* Best season: Spring to autumn; the shaded river path stays cool even on hot summer days. 🍃
* Bail-out point: [Hatonosu Station / 鳩ノ巣駅][hatonosu-station] sits partway along the route, so you can stop there early or use it as a shorter start. 🚉
* ⚠️ The stretch between Kori Station and Hatonosu Station includes long paved-road sections, so watch for cars.
* ⚠️ The rocks in Hatonosu Valley are very slippery when wet, so wear shoes with good grip.
* ⚠️ Bears live around Shiromaru Lake, so carry a bear bell or make noise as you walk.


## Trail route 🥾

* Kori Station -> Suniwa Bridge -> Hatonosu Valley -> Shiromaru Dam -> Kazuma Gorge -> OkuTama Station. 🚶
* The route also works in reverse, from OkuTama Station to Kori Station.
* My recorded hikes on YAMAP:
  * [Otama Walking Trail - June 2024 hike][my-yamap-2024] 🗻
  * [OkuTama river walk on a hot summer day - August 2025 hike][my-yamap-2025] 🗻

Metrics:

* Duration: 4:35 to 6:15
* Distance: 9.4 km to 9.7 km
* Elevation gain ↗️: 601 m to 624 m
* Elevation decline ↘️: 544 m to 571 m
* Trail type: Point to point (one way)

The [June 2024 hike][my-yamap-2024] recorded 6:15, 9.7 km, 601 m of gain, and 544 m of decline. The [August 2025 hike][my-yamap-2025] recorded 4:35, 9.4 km, 624 m of gain, and 571 m of decline; its GPS track ends at Shiromaru Station / `白丸駅`, one station before OkuTama Station.

## Shorter version: skip the paved road 🚶

Start at Hatonosu Station instead of Kori Station to skip most of the paved-road walking.

* [My September 2025 record][my-yamap-short] starts next to Hatonosu Station / `鳩ノ巣駅` and ends at OkuTama Station / `奥多摩駅`; the record lists no checkpoints, so the start and end come from its GPS start and finish points.
* It drops the stretch between Kori Station and Hatonosu Station, which holds most of the paved-road walking of the full route.
* You still pass the highlights: Hatonosu Valley, Shiromaru Dam, and the Kazuma Gorge walkway.

Metrics:

* Duration: 3:45
* Distance: 7.2 km
* Elevation gain ↗️: 290 m
* Elevation decline ↘️: 261 m
* Trail type: Point to point (one way)

Food options along this trail are few, so bring lunch and snacks with you. 🍙

* [Harashima Shokudo][harashima-shokudo] / `はらしま食堂` - a diner right next to Kori Station. 🍜 <!-- TODO: verify opening days and hours -->
* [Hatonosu-so][hatonosu-so] / `はとのす荘` - a riverside lodge above Hatonosu Valley. 🏨 <!-- TODO: verify whether day visitors can eat lunch here -->

## Key photo spots 📸

* Giant inugusu tree / `古里附のイヌグス` 🌳  
  One of the largest trees of its kind in Japan, squeezed between the train line and the road near Kori Station.

* [Suniwa Bridge][suniwa-bridge] / `寸庭橋` 🌉  
  The crossing to the quiet riverside path, with the small Upper Falls / `上の滝` just beyond.

* [Soryu Falls][soryu-falls] / `双竜の滝` 💧  
  A waterfall right behind Hatonosu Station, where the water falls in two dragon-like streams.

* [Hatonosu suspension bridge][hatonosu-bridge] / `鳩ノ巣小橋` 🌉  
  A small suspension bridge at the entrance to the rocky gorge.

* [Hatonosu Valley][hatonosu-valley] / `鳩ノ巣渓谷` 📸  
  One of the 30 selected views of the Tama River; the name means "dove's nest".

* [Shiromaru Dam][shiromaru-dam] / `白丸ダム` and [Shiromaru Lake][shiromaru-lake] / `白丸湖` 💧  
  The dam has a fish ladder, and a calm walkway follows the lake behind it.
