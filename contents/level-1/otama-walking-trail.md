## Getting there 🚂

Start station: [Kori Station / 古里駅][kori-sta] (JR Ome Line) 🚉

Finish station: [OkuTama Station / 奥多摩駅][okutama-sta] (JR Ome Line) 🚉

Travel from Shinjuku: about 1 hr 40 min ⏱️

* Ride the JR Chuo Line from Shinjuku toward Tachikawa, continue on the JR Ome Line toward OkuTama, and get off at Kori Station. 🚆
* The walking route starts a 5-minute walk from Kori Station.

> [!TIP]
> Try taking the Holiday Rapid Okutama (休日快速 おくたま) train to get to Ome station faster from Shinjuku!


## Trail overview 🥾

* Difficulty: Easy - great for beginners! 🟢
* Toilets: Use the toilet at Kori Station before you start; [Ecotto Shiromaru][ecotto-shiromaru] at Shiromaru Dam has another one later on. 🚻
* Drink: Bring plenty of water but there are a few cafes and stores along the way.
* Convenience store: [7-Eleven][7-eleven] near Kori station
* Lunch: Few [food options][food-options] along the trail, so pack your own. 🍱
* Hiking poles 🔧: 2 / 5
  * Not needed; the route is a riverside walkway with steps, not a mountain trail.
* Trail running 👟: 3 / 5
  * Mostly runnable with a few narrow and rocky sections in the Hatonosu Valley.
* Best season: Spring to autumn; the shaded river path stays cool even on hot summer days. 🍃
* Bail-out point: [Hatonosu Station / 鳩ノ巣駅][hatonosu-sta] sits partway along the route, so you can stop there early or use it as a shorter start. 🚉
* ⚠️ The stretch between Kori Station and Hatonosu Station includes long paved-road sections, so watch for cars.
* ⚠️ The rocks in Hatonosu Valley are very slippery when wet, so wear shoes with good grip.


## Trail route 🥾

* Kori Station -> Suniwa Bridge -> Hatonosu Valley -> Shiromaru Dam -> Kazuma Gorge -> OkuTama Station.
* The route also works in reverse, from OkuTama Station to Kori Station.
* My recorded hikes on YAMAP:
  * [Otama Walking Trail - June 2024 hike][my-yamap-2024] 🗻
  * [OkuTama river walk on a hot summer day - August 2025 hike][my-yamap-2025] 🗻

Metrics:

* Duration: 4:35 to 6:15
* Distance: 9.4 km to 9.7 km
* Elevation gain ↗: 601 m to 624 m
* Elevation decline ↘: 544 m to 571 m
* Trail type: Point to point (one way)

The [June 2024 hike][my-yamap-2024] recorded 6:15, 9.7 km, 601 m of gain, and 544 m of decline.
The [August 2025 hike][my-yamap-2025] recorded 4:35, 9.4 km, 624 m of gain, and 571 m of decline; its GPS track ends at Shiromaru Station / `白丸駅`, one station before OkuTama Station.

How to read the map:

* Each sheet places the downstream end on the right, so walking upstream from Kori Station to OkuTama Station means reading each sheet from right to left, starting with the eastern half above.
* The circled numbers match the numbers printed on the trail signposts.
* The map gives 3.4 km from Kori Station to Hatonosu, 2.2 km from Hatonosu to Shiromaru, and 2.6 km from Shiromaru to OkuTama Station, for 8.2 km and about 3 hours in total. This covers the signposted trail only, which is shorter than the full walk recorded above.

## Shorter version: skip the paved road

Start at Hatonosu Station instead of Kori Station to skip most of the paved-road walking.

* [My September 2025 record][my-yamap-short] starts next to Hatonosu Station / `鳩ノ巣駅` and ends at OkuTama Station / `奥多摩駅`; the record lists no checkpoints, so the start and end come from its GPS start and finish points.
* It drops the stretch between Kori Station and Hatonosu Station, which holds most of the paved-road walking of the full route.
* You still pass the highlights: Hatonosu Valley, Shiromaru Dam, and the Kazuma Gorge walkway.

Metrics:

* Duration: 3:45
* Distance: 7.2 km
* Elevation gain ↗: 290 m
* Elevation decline ↘: 261 m
* Trail type: Point to point (one way)

## Food 🍱

Food options along this trail are few, so bring lunch and snacks with you. 🍙

* [Harashima Shokudo][harashima-shokudo] / `はらしま食堂` - a diner right next to Kori Station. 🍜 <!-- TODO: verify opening days and hours -->
* [Hatonosu-so][hatonosu-so] / `はとのす荘` - a riverside lodge above Hatonosu Valley. ♨️ There is no lunch for day visitors, but you can use the [day-time hot spring bath][hatonosu-so-onsen] from 11:30 to 15:00, with last entry at 14:30, for 1,370 yen per adult.
* [Ecotto Shiromaru][ecotto-shiromaru] / `エコっと白丸` - a free public exhibit hall at [Shiromaru Dam][shiromaru-dam] about hydropower, with a toilet, vending machines, and ice cream. 🍦
* [Moegi no Yu][moegi-no-yu] / `もえぎの湯` - a hot spring facility just before OkuTama Station, a nice way to end the hike. ♨️


## Key photo spots 📸

* [Giant inugusu tree][giant-inugusu-tree] / `古里附のイヌグス` 🌳  
  One of the largest trees of its kind in Japan, squeezed between the train line and the road near Kori Station.

* [Suniwa Bridge][suniwa-bridge] / `寸庭橋` 🌉  
  The crossing to the quiet riverside path, with the small Upper Falls / `上の滝` just beyond.

* [Soryu Falls][soryu-falls] / `双竜の滝` 💧  
  A waterfall right behind Hatonosu Station, where the water falls in two dragon-like streams.

* [Hatonosu suspension bridge][hatonosu-bridge] / `鳩ノ巣小橋` 🌉  
  A small suspension bridge at the entrance to the rocky gorge.

* [Hatonosu Valley][hatonosu-valley] / `鳩ノ巣渓谷` 📸  
  One of the 30 selected views of the Tama River; the name means "dove's nest".

* [Shiromaru Dam][shiromaru-dam] / `白丸ダム` and [Shiromaru Lake][shiromaru-lake] / `白丸湖` 💧  
  The dam has a fish ladder, and a calm walkway follows the lake behind it.
  You can also go a bit inside the dam from the [Shiromaru Dam promenade][shiromaru-walkway] / `白丸ダム遊歩道`, where a spiral staircase drops about 27 m to the fish ladder, one of the largest in Japan at 330 m long.
  Entry is free, and the staircase usually opens on weekends, public holidays, and during the summer holidays. <!-- TODO: verify the opening days and hours -->
