## Getting there 🚂

Start station: [Kori Station][kori-sta] (JR Ome Line) 🚉

Finish station: [OkuTama Station][okutama-sta] (JR Ome Line) 🚉

Travel from Shinjuku: about 1 hr 40 min ⏱️

* Ride the JR Chuo Line from Shinjuku toward Tachikawa, continue on the JR Ome Line toward OkuTama, and get off at Kori Station. 🚆
* The walking route starts a 5-minute walk from Kori Station.

> [!TIP]
> Try taking the Holiday Rapid Okutama (休日快速 おくたま) train to get to Ome station faster from Shinjuku!


## Trail overview 📋

* Difficulty: Easy - great for beginners! 🟢
* Toilets: Use the toilet at Kori Station before you start; [Ecotto Shiromaru][ecotto-shiromaru] at [Shiromaru Dam][shiromaru-dam] has another one later on. 🚻
* Drink: Bring plenty of water but there are a few cafes and stores along the way.
* Convenience store: [7-Eleven][7-eleven] near Kori station
* Lunch: Few [food options][food-options] along the trail, so pack your own. 🍱
* Hiking poles 🔧: 2 / 5
  * Not needed; the route is a riverside walkway with steps, not a mountain trail.
* Trail running 👟: 3 / 5
  * Mostly runnable with a few narrow and rocky sections in the [Hatonosu Valley][hatonosu-valley].
* Best season: Spring to autumn; the shaded river path stays cool even on hot summer days. 🍃
* Bail-out point: [Hatonosu Station / `鳩ノ巣駅`][hatonosu-sta] sits partway along the route, so you can stop there early or use it as a shorter start. 🚉
* ⚠️ The stretch between Kori Station and Hatonosu Station includes long paved-road sections, so watch for cars.
* ⚠️ The rocks in Hatonosu Valley are very slippery when wet, so wear shoes with good grip.


## Trail route 🥾

1. [Kori Station / `古里駅`][kori-sta]
2. [Suniwa Bridge / `寸庭橋`][suniwa-bridge]
3. [Hatonosu Valley / `鳩ノ巣渓谷`][hatonosu-valley]
4. [Shiromaru Dam / `白丸ダム`][shiromaru-dam]
5. [Kazuma Gorge / `一ノ瀬渓谷`][kazuma-gorge]
6. [OkuTama Station / `奥多摩駅`][okutama-sta]

* The route also works in reverse, from OkuTama Station to Kori Station.
* My recorded hikes on YAMAP:
  * [Otama Walking Trail - June 2024 hike][my-yamap-2024] ~6 hr, 9.7 km 🗻
  * [OkuTama river walk on a hot summer day - August 2025 hike][my-yamap-2025] ~4.5hr, 9.4 km 🗻

Metrics:

* Duration: 4:35 to 6:15
* Distance: 9.4 km to 9.7 km
* Elevation gain ↗: 601 m to 624 m
* Elevation decline ↘: 544 m to 571 m
* Trail type: Point to point (one way)

## Shortcuts ✂️


### Skipping the paved section between Kori station and Shotanba parking lot

Unfortunately, the stretch between [Kori Station][kori-sta] and the [Shotanba Sightseeing Parking Lot / `小丹波観光駐車場`][shotanba-parking] is a 17-minute walk on a paved road.

To avoid this, start from [Hatonosu Station][hatonosu-sta] instead for a shorter hike, though you will encounter dirt trails within 5 minutes.


### Skipping the road between Lake Shiromaru and OkuTama station

When you pass [Earth Garden / `森の中のお肉レストラン アースガーデン`][earth-garden], the route becomes a local village road until you reach OkuTama Station.

To avoid this, cross the bridge near Earth Garden to head to [Shiromaru Station / `白丸駅`][shiromaru-sta], then take the train to [OkuTama Station][okutama-sta].


### Minimum pavement route

* [My September 2025 record][my-yamap-short] starts at [Hatonosu Station][hatonosu-sta], hike to [Shiromaru Station / `白丸駅`][shiromaru-sta], then take the train to [OkuTama Station][okutama-sta] and then walk around the OkuTama station rea.
* You still see the highlights: [Hatonosu Valley][hatonosu-valley], [Shiromaru Dam][shiromaru-dam], and the [Kazuma Gorge][kazuma-gorge] walkway.

> [!TIP] Nice riverside paths near OkuTama station
> Walk towards [Tokei Bridge / `登計橋`][tokei-bridge] for nice views of the river.

## Food 🍱

Food options along this trail are few, so bring lunch and snacks with you. 🍙

* [Harashima Shokudo / `はらしま食堂`][harashima-shokudo] - a diner right next to Kori Station. 🍜 <!-- TODO: verify opening days and hours -->
* [Hatonosu-so / `はとのす荘`][hatonosu-so] - a riverside lodge above Hatonosu Valley. ♨️ There is no lunch for day visitors, but you can use the [day-time hot spring bath][hatonosu-so-onsen] from 11:30 to 15:00, with last entry at 14:30, for 1,370 yen per adult.
* [Ecotto Shiromaru / `エコっと白丸`][ecotto-shiromaru] - a free public exhibit hall at [Shiromaru Dam][shiromaru-dam] about hydropower, with a toilet, vending machines, and ice cream. 🍦
* [Moegi no Yu / `もえぎの湯`][moegi-no-yu] - a hot spring facility just before OkuTama Station, a nice way to end the hike. ♨️


## Key photo spots 📸

* [Giant inugusu tree / `古里附のイヌグス`][giant-inugusu-tree] 🌳  
  One of the largest trees of its kind in Japan, squeezed between the train line and the road near Kori Station.

* [Suniwa Bridge][suniwa-bridge] 🌉  
  The crossing to the quiet riverside path, with the small Upper Falls / `上の滝` just beyond.

* [Soryu Falls / `双竜の滝`][soryu-falls] 💧  
  A waterfall right behind Hatonosu Station, where the water falls in two dragon-like streams.

* [Hatonosu suspension bridge / `鳩ノ巣小橋`][hatonosu-bridge] 🌉  
  A small suspension bridge at the entrance to the rocky gorge.

* [Hatonosu Valley][hatonosu-valley] 📸  
  One of the 30 selected views of the Tama River; the name means "dove's nest".

* [Shiromaru Dam][shiromaru-dam] and [Shiromaru Lake][shiromaru-lake] 💧  
  The dam has a fish ladder, and a calm walkway follows the lake behind it.
  You can also go a bit inside the dam from the [Shiromaru Dam promenade / `白丸ダム遊歩道`][shiromaru-walkway], where a spiral staircase drops about 27 m to the fish ladder, one of the largest in Japan at 330 m long.
  Entry is free, and the staircase usually opens on weekends, public holidays, and during the summer holidays. <!-- TODO: verify the opening days and hours -->
