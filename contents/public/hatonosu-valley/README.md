# Images in the `hatonosu-valley` folder

Photos for [contents/level-1/hatonosu-valley.ja.md](../../level-1/hatonosu-valley.ja.md), the Japanese Hatonosu Valley page.


## Source and copyright

These photos are from Tachikawa Online's Hatonosu walk page.
They are not mine, and I do not own the copyright to them.
I am using them to keep the original page as a record of the route, and to provide visual context.
The original page is at <https://www.tachikawaonline.jp/walk/hatonosu.htm>.

The full rights notice, the reporter credit, and the takedown offer live on the Japanese page itself, which is the verbatim reproduction.
The English page carries only a `Source:` line by choice; that asymmetry is deliberate, not an oversight.
